# Page Flipper & Timeline Scrubbing

The Page Flipper / Timeline Scrubber (`Key B`) allows readers to casually flip through sentences and scenes like a physical book.

---

## 📖 Live Scrubbing & Previews

- **Interactive Timeline Slider**: Dragging the scrubber slides across the entire instruction sequence of the current chapter.
- **Floating Preview Box**: Shows the snippet of dialogue text, speaker name, and line number under the scrubber cursor before committing the jump.
- **Choice Markers**: Important interactive branch points are marked along the timeline track for quick reference.
